OUR PURPOSE

Why PracCode?

Learning takes practice, and every small improvement brings you closer to your goals.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide beginners with an approachable space where they can practice typing coding-related text, improve their accuracy, and become more confident through consistent practice.

🚀

Our Vision

We envision a learning environment where beginners feel encouraged to practice, recognize their progress, and continue developing their skills at their own pace.
